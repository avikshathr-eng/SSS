01 · The studio

One studio, from first line to final frame.

Structure and Sky Studios is a pre-construction visualization studio. We turn plan sets and BIM models into the marketing assets that sell, finance and approve a project before it is built: photoreal stills, 3D walkthroughs, drone and aerial capture, sales films and interactive tours.

You deal with one vendor, under one contract and one schedule. We own the narrative and the on-site work, so the story, the model and the aerial plates are made to fit each other.

How a plan becomes a launch

1Plans and brief. We review your plan set, models and target audience, and confirm scope in writing.
2Model. We build the 3D model from your drawings. The model is reviewed and then locked.
3Clay review. Cameras and composition are approved on clay views before final rendering begins.
4Final production. Final renders, animation, aerial composites or film are produced and reviewed within the revision rounds of your package.
5Delivery. Final files are released on receipt of the final instalment, with the usage licence described in clause 9 of the terms.

Agreement between the parties

These terms form part of the proposal. When the client signs the proposal, the proposal and these terms become a binding agreement between Structure and Sky Studios (“the Studio”) and the client named on the cover (“the Client”).

1. Services and scope

The Studio will provide only the services and deliverables described in Section 03 (“the Services”). Anything not expressly listed is outside scope. The Services are creative visualization services. They are not architectural, engineering, surveying, legal, planning or construction services, and the Studio does not certify or verify any design.

2. Client materials and responsibility

The Client will supply complete, accurate and current plans, models, specifications, finishes and references on time. The Studio relies on these materials as given and is not responsible for errors, omissions or conflicts in them. The Client confirms that it owns, or has permission to use, everything it supplies, including any third-party names, logos, marks, artwork, likenesses and property, and that its use for the Services does not infringe any right of any person.

3. Artistic representation, not a statement of fact

Renders, animations, films and interactive tours are artistic interpretations of a design that may not yet exist. They are illustrative only. They are not construction documents, are not a warranty or promise of how the finished project will look, perform or be dimensioned, and must not be used for construction or to settle any dispute over design. The Client is solely responsible for the content of its advertising and marketing, for including any disclaimers or disclosures that applicable law or regulation requires (including real estate, advertising and consumer-protection rules), and for the accuracy of what it says to buyers, tenants, lenders and authorities.

4. Revisions and approvals

The number of revision rounds is stated in Section 03. A round is one consolidated set of written comments. Approval of the model, the clay views or any other stage is final for that stage. After model lock, changes to design, layout, materials, camera positions or story are change orders under clause 5, even if the work is already underway.

5. Change orders

Any request outside the agreed scope, or beyond the included revision rounds, will be quoted in writing before work begins. Unless a fixed price is agreed, additional design work is billed at $110 to $150 per hour, as stated in the change order. Work on a change order starts only after the Client approves it in writing, and it may move the schedule.

6. Schedule and delay

Dates are good-faith targets and are not guaranteed. They depend on timely receipt of materials, feedback, approvals and payment. Delay by the Client, or by events outside the Studio’s reasonable control, extends the schedule accordingly. The Studio is not liable for loss caused by delay, and time is not of the essence.

7. Fees and payment

All fees are in United States dollars (USD) and exclude taxes, which the Client pays where they apply. Fifty percent (50%) is due in advance on signing, before work begins. The remaining fifty percent (50%) is due before delivery of the final files. The Studio may pause work while any invoice is unpaid. Overdue amounts bear interest at [1.5% per month] or the highest rate the law allows, whichever is lower, and the Client pays reasonable costs of collection, including legal fees. The Client may not withhold or set off payment because of a dispute about other matters.

8. Deposit, cancellation and refunds

The advance payment secures the Studio’s schedule and is non-refundable once work has begun. If the Client cancels or suspends the project, the Client pays for all work performed and costs committed up to that date, and for any stage that has begun, up to the full fee. Unused prepaid credits expire 90 days after issue, roll over once, and have no cash value.

9. Ownership and licence

On receipt of full payment, the Studio grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the final deliverables to market and promote the named project only. Resale, sub-licensing, use for any other project, and use in third-party advertising networks or stock libraries are not permitted and are priced separately. The Studio keeps ownership of all intellectual property in its working files, 3D models, scenes, materials, assets, tools, techniques and unused concepts, and none of it passes to the Client unless a separate written agreement says so. Until full payment is received, the Client has no licence to use any deliverable, draft or preview.

10. Portfolio and credit

The Studio may display the deliverables, and non-confidential behind-the-scenes material, in its portfolio, website, awards entries and marketing, and may identify the project by a descriptive title. The Client may ask in writing, before delivery, that the Client’s name or the project address be withheld.
